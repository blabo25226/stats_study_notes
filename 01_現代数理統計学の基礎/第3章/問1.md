# 第3章 問1

## 問題の要約

離散一様分布の確率母関数を求め、それを用いて平均と分散を求める。

## 答案

$X \sim \mathrm{Udiscrete}(N)$ より
$$
f_X(x) = \frac{1}{N}, \quad x = 1, 2, \dots, N, \quad (N \in \mathbb{N},\ N \geq 1)
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
G_X(s) &= E(s^X) \\
&= \sum_{x=1}^N s^x \frac{1}{N} \\
&= \frac{1}{N} \cdot s \cdot \frac{1 - s^N}{1 - s}, \quad 1 - s > 0 \\
&= \frac{s}{N} \cdot \frac{1 - s^N}{1 - s}, \quad 1 > s
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
G_X(s) &= \frac{1}{N}(s + s^2 + s^3 + \cdots + s^N) \\
G_X'(s) &= \frac{1}{N}(1 + 2s + 3s^2 + \cdots + N s^{N-1}) \\
G_X'(1) &= \frac{1}{N}(1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + N) \\
&= \frac{1}{N} \cdot \frac{N}{2}(N+1) = \frac{N+1}{2}
\end{align*}
$$

よって
$$
G_X'(1) = E(X \cdot 1^{X-1}) = E(X) = \frac{N+1}{2}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
G_X''(s) &= \frac{1}{N}(0 + 2 + 6s + \cdots + N(N-1)s^{N-2}) \\
G_X''(1) &= \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^N k(k-1) \\
&= \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^N (k^2 - k) \\
&= \frac{1}{N}\left( \frac{N}{6}(N+1)(2N+1) - \frac{N}{2}(N+1) \right) \\
&= \left( \frac{1}{6}(2N+1) - \frac{1}{2} \right)(N+1) \\
&= \left( \frac{N}{3} + \frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{2} \right)(N+1) \\
&= \left( \frac{N}{3} + \frac{1-3}{6} \right)(N+1) \\
&= \left( \frac{N}{3} - \frac{1}{3} \right)(N+1) \\
&= \frac{1}{3}(N+1)(N-1)
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
G_X''(1) &= E(X(X-1)s^{X-2})\big|_{s=1} \\
&= E(X(X-1)) \\
&= E(X^2) - E(X)
\end{align*}
$$

よって
$$
E(X^2) = \frac{1}{3}(N+1)(N-1) + \frac{N+1}{2}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
V(X) &= \frac{1}{3}(N+1)(N-1) + \frac{N+1}{2} - \frac{(N+1)^2}{4} \\
&= (N+1)\left( \frac{N}{3} - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{N}{4} - \frac{1}{4} \right) \\
&= (N+1)\left( \frac{4-3}{12}N + \frac{-4+6-3}{12} \right) \\
&= (N+1)\left( \frac{N}{12} - \frac{1}{12} \right) \\
&= \frac{1}{12}(N+1)(N-1)
\end{align*}
$$
